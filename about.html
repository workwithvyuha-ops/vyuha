From the Founder

The Struggle & The Spark

Hi, I am Sourav, the founder and head storyteller of Vyuha Creation. My romance with the camera did not start in a fancy classroom or a studio. It began in the busy cultural lanes of my college, carrying a crop-sensor entry-level DSLR and photographing anything that had a story to tell.

In the beginning, it was hard. Getting client trust without a portfolio was a giant mountain. I worked as an assistant for months, carrying lights, standing in the rain, learning the rhythm of wedding rituals, and understanding how corporate schedules function.

When the pandemic hit in 2020, everything went dark. But instead of quitting, I treated it as a crucible. I locked myself in a room for 10 hours a day, studying Hollywood color grading, mastering complex editing structures, and testing dynamic camera rigs. Vyuha Creation was born out of that absolute dedication. Today, we are not just a photographer; we are a tight-knit family of cinematic creators who love visual engineering.

Our Milestones

The Vyuha Chronicles

A chronological journey of our creative growth, artistic transitions, and major breakthroughs.

2018

The Spark

Picked up the first second-hand camera. Shot small university gatherings, student theater plays, and local street grids, developing an eye for candid, authentic facial expressions.

2020

The Crucible

Faced absolute zero bookings. Spent 12 months in lockdown self-learning cinematic audio design, lighting arrays, advanced DaVinci Resolve grading, and narrative structure.

2022

The Birth of Vyuha

Officially launched "Vyuha Creation" as an independent visual crew. Booked our first three premium weddings and established our signature warm, cinematic-gold aesthetic.

2025

Expanding Horizons

Grew the crew to 10+ visual engineers. Integrated advanced dual-pilot heavy drone operations, professional stabilizer rigs, and entered high-scale corporate events coverage.
